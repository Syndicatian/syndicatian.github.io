About

I am a university student interested in the institutions, ideas, and systems that shape human societies over long periods of time.

My interests include political economy, demographics, international affairs, technological change, and the future of civilization. I am particularly interested in how societies respond to large-scale challenges such as population decline, artificial intelligence, and space expansion.

This website serves as a collection of my essays, research projects, analytical work, and creative projects.

One of these projects is Niusiv, a speculative worldbuilding setting used to explore questions of governance, identity, continuity, and human development through fiction.

  • Demographics
  • Space Governance
  • International Relations
