Niusiv

Niusiv is a speculative civilization project exploring questions of governance, continuity, identity, technology, and long-term societal development.

Rather than functioning as a traditional fictional setting, Niusiv serves as a framework for examining how future societies might organize themselves under conditions of resource constraints, demographic pressure, technological transformation, and ideological conflict.

Core Themes

  • Continuity and Immortality
  • Universality and Culture
  • Centralization and Governance
  • Technology and Human Identity
  • The Future of Civilization
